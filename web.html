Chapter 1: rip off that bandaid even if you tear the phantom wound apart

Chapter Text

Before Stephen Strange erased the ties between the identity of Spiderman and Peter Parker in 2024, Peter knew he had to say the inevitable goodbye to the people who meant so much in his life.

Aunt May was dead, killed by a stupid green ass goblin. Peter wished that she was alive. Being alive but not remembering your nephew was better than being 6 feet in the ground.

He had always been an introverted kid at school and did not have many friends. However, he did have two best friends who he could trust his life with: MJ, his girlfriend and Ned, his man-in-the-chair and his best friend.

There was one more person who he owed his life to, and that was Tony Stark, the billionaire who had treated Peter like his own son.

﹌﹌﹌﹌﹌﹌﹌﹌

Peter swung through the air, the familiar pull of his webs carrying him toward the two figures waiting at the base of the Statue of Liberty.

For a moment, everything felt normal. It felt like a regular day with his best friends in the entire world. The sun was shining but there was silence in the city. A silence as if to mourn all the people who had died.

“Peter!”

MJ and Ned turned toward him just as he landed, and the relief on their faces made something inside Peter’s heart ache. MJ immediately ran toward him, wrapping her arms around him. Ned followed, and for a few seconds, the three of them stood together in a desperate tight hug. Peter heard the agitated increased heartbeat of his friends and swallowed hard.

When they finally pulled apart, Peter had looked between them, scanning every inch of their body for harm. His own body was bruised, dirtied, and sore but the safety of his friends came first.

“You guys okay?”

MJ nodded, though Peter had immediately noticed the cut above her eye. His heart clenched at the sight of his friends, who were innocent people, hurt because of something that he had caused. They didn't deserve this, to have to deal with both physical and emotional wounds just because their friend Peter Parker was Spiderman.

“Oh my God. You're bleeding.” There was a small gash on her forehead, likely caused from falling debris.

“I'm fine,” she said quickly. “I'm okay.”

Peter had touched her face gently, inspecting the cut with trembling fingers.

“You sure?”

“I promise.”

Peter nodded, forcing himself to believe her.

“Okay.”

Peter's smile disappeared.

He knew he had to deliver the news. Something he had been dreading since the moment Strange had told him to say goodbye.

“You're gonna forget who I am.”

Ned stared at him.

“What?”

MJ's expression changed immediately.

“Forget who you are? What are you talking about?” Peter could tell she tried to sound calm but panic laced her tone.

Peter swallowed. There was no easy way to rip off the bandaid, the bandaid that had protected him from bullies like Flash and brought him moments of distraction so Peter could slip away to save civilians.

“It's okay. I'm gonna come find you. I'll explain everything. I'll make you remember me.” Peter's voice quivered.

Ned's eyes widened and he stared in shock before he began to shake his head in disbelief.

"it's okay," Peter's voice wavered, "I'm gonna come find you, it'll be like this never happened. I'll make you remember me, okay?"

MJ and Ned still stared in disbelief

"But w-what if it doesn't work?" MJ stammered, her usual confident tone gone and replaced with fear, "w-what if we can't remember you?" She spoke everything that Peter had feared, what if they didn't remember him?

She and Ned shared grieved glances as she tried to think of something they could do.

"There's nothing we can do." Peter rasped. "It'll be okay."

"Promise?" Ned choked out.

"Promise."

Then for the last time in their time together, Peter and Ned did their signature handshake, before embracing in a tight hug. Ned's eyes filled with tears and he sobbed as he held onto his best friend tightly. It would be the last time he embraced his best friend.

MJ's were red rimmed and pooled with tears as she cupped Peter's face with her hands. She shakily joked that he better come find them or she would do everything to find him. Thunder had rumbled overhead despite the clear day, a sign for them to hurry up.

Then for the final time, Peter kissed her.

Peter knew he had one more person to say goodbye to.

His heart ached so so so much, how much he yearned to run back to Strange and tell him to cancel the spell, to change it, anything. He was only a teenager, he didn't want to lose his friends yet. His parents were gone, his aunt and uncle were gone, and now he had to lose the last people who cared about him.

But he knew deep down that this was for the best, that nobody remembered him.

When he reached the 80th floor of Stark Tower, Tony Stark was waiting for him. He looked out the window into the destroyed city of New York. Bright blue and red lights from police cars around local establishments that had been destroyed could be seen from SI.

When Peter walked in, Tony didn’t turn around; he recognized that it was Peter from his footsteps. They had spent so much time together, how could he not remember how Peter’s footsteps sounded?

"Strange told me what you needed to do." Tony said as Peter approached. He still stared outside. Two birds swooped together on the horizon.

"yeah." Peter whispered.

Tony Stark turned around and Peter saw why he had decided not to face him when he walked in. His face was similarly dirtied by blood and dirt from the battle, his clothes also ripped in places. His eyes were red and bloodshot. Tony looked like he didn't sleep in a week.

There was a moment of silence. Tony sniffled and looked up at the young boy standing before him. He was only 17 but the universe had played its cruel cards onto the table.

"Come here Roos" Tony said at last, opening his arms.

Peter embraced him tightly, taking in the last whiff of the familiar scent of Tony Stark. Motor oil, expensive cologne, and the warm smell of home.

"Thank you, Mr. Stark." he whispered, "for everything." Over the course of his high school years, Tony had fed Peter, given him a home at Stark Tower, and had been there for him. He had always rooted for Peter when he was nervous to ask MJ out, he had been there to console Peter after the death of May, and most importantly, he had placed his own hopes and dreams onto Peter.

"Come find me after, okay Roos?"

"Yeah" Peter had whispered before he pulled out of the embrace. Tony’s face was full of worry as he stared at Peter.

“I’ll come fix this and it’ll be like none of this ever happened.”

﹌﹌﹌﹌﹌﹌﹌﹌

“Do it” Peter nodded at Strange floating above the Statue of Liberty.

Stephen Strange put his hands together and orange runes lit up in a circle around him. They spun around, and the circle got wider and wider.

People not from their dimension began to fade. Doc Ock looked up at the sky, a small smile on his lips as registered the orange cirle of magic before he faded. Spiderman 2 and Spiderman 3 looked at each other. They also knew their time was up. They flashed each other the peace sign before both of them faded away.

The glowing circle of orange runes rapidly expanded before it exploded over the city, finishing the spell.

Peter ripped off the bandaid, Strange cast the spell, and the world forgot Peter Parker ever existed.

Peter Parker had tried to approach MJ and Ned, he really did. He waited a couple days before he went to the coffee shop where MJ worked, ready to make it all right again. After all, he had promised that he would find her.

It was going to be easy peasy, he would go up to MJ, read her the letter he had written, she would remember him, they would be in love again, and everything would go back to normal, that’s how it’ll go he told himself. Same with Ned and Mr. Stark, he would tell them all about what they had forgotten and then he would go back to building Star Wars Lego sets at Ned’s and working in the lab with Mr. Stark.

However his hopes came crashing down soon enough.

Peter Parker knew that MJ had forgotten who he was, but definitely did not expect to be treated like a total stranger. MJ had the same polite smile she gave to all her customers and Peter was no different than another guy who walked in on a Tuesday afternoon wanting a coffee. Even Flash Thompson would have been treated with more emotion and recognition.

“Hi, my name is Peter Parker and…” he trailed off. MJ gave him a bemused stare.

He stared at her, at the girl who he loved. She stared back, at a total stranger staring at her with such pain in his eyes.

Creepy. At least he wasn’t some old ass man.

In the end he couldn’t do it, all he did was make a fool of himself. He clutched the letter he wrote to her tightly in his hand, scrunching it up.

When he walked out of the coffee shop, away from the girl who he had loved, he left a piece of his broken heart there. Reality hit like a blow to his heart, what did he expect anyways?

Peter had known that everyone would forget him but he didn’t expect it to hurt so bad. How could he make his friends remember him when their past had been scrubbed to nothing?

Well, that didn’t work but you miss 100% of the shots you don’t take.

Peter had also promised to come find Mr. Stark after. His mind unbiddenly conjured up an image of Tony Stark looking at this random ass kid who claimed to borderline be his personal intern or even son with disgust and confusion. The same look he had given Flash when Tony learned that Flash was bullying Peter at school.

He faltered in his step.

No, he had promised Tony that he would come find him, and while Peter Parker knew it wasn’t going to work, he still had to give it a shot.

It wasn’t a long walk to SI. Well, maybe it was but Peter wanted to get some fresh air after what just happened. His stomach turned as he approached the daunting tower.

Huh, he didn’t remember SI being such a huge tower.

Peter craned his head up yet he still couldn’t see the tippy top of the tower. He knew that there were 93 floors but it really seemed like there were 193 floors. The tower loomed over him, casting him in its shadow. The same tower that he had lived in and worked in and made his home never seemed so imposing.

“Hi, I was wondering if I could see Mr. Stark?” He asked the receptionist. She was new, the name tag on her breast read ‘Marie’ in neat white letters against a black background.

She stared at him. Peter’s fingers nervously drummed against his leg.

“Do you have an appointment?” Marie flipped through the book for Tony’s appointments. It was old and tattered, the pages full with pen ink and white out. Peter remembered when he had first met Mr. Stark, and the receptionist had written his name, Peter Benjamin Parker down in a slot for 4:30 in the afternoon with a blue ballpoint pen. He had felt so nervous but at the same time so full of excitement.

“N-no,” Peter stammered.

“Sorry, you need to have an appointment with Mr. Stark to see him, he’s extremely busy” Her tone was apologetic but none of the sympathy in her voice reached her facial expressions. He was just another person who thought they could promote their product to Mr. Stark himself, no doubt.

Peter knew Mr. Stark wasn’t busy. He was probably tinkering away in his lab again.

Right, how was he going to get in anyways? He no longer had an internship because obviously nobody remembered him. He really never had an internship before anyways. Peter Parker and Spiderman were considered family, not some worker that made repairs in the lab and then got out of there. And if nobody remembered the ties between Peter and the anonymous masked neighborhood crime stopping vigilante, nobody remembered Peter.

Sometimes Spider-Man has to do the hard thing, even if it breaks Peter Parker’s heart.

Heart heavy, he thanked her and left.

They were all better off without him, really.

Ned and MJ had both gotten into MIT, their dream school. They didn’t need some random Peter Parker dragging their leg behind, they deserved this opportunity. After all the chaos that Spiderman had put them through, he could at least owe them this peace. Shelter from the dangers of the risk of being associated with Spiderman.

Peter knew he wouldn’t get into MIT, not with all his records basically scrubbed. He had to apply to a community college as a transfer kid from another country. It broke his heart to see all his academic progress and achievements he had made over the years gone. His record was now a blank state, no Academic Decathlon, no honor role, no grades being the top of his class.

He hated it at Queen’s community college.

The coursework there was dead easy. Back then, with Tony’s help, he was able to enroll in online MIT courses. The same courses that Ned or MJ might be taking at this very moment.

He had no friends. A lot of the people who he did see at Queens from his high school had no recollection of who he was.

Truth is, Peter was scared of making friends. He shrugged off classmate after classmate when they invited him to study sessions, sports games, or even play a round of Nintendo Super Smash Bros in their dorms. They got the message quickly and by the mid of first semester he got no more invites; Peter Parker was officially a loner.

Tony Stark and his crew were better off without him anyways. There was no more kid he needed to wake up at 2am for because he got stabbed. Happy no longer needed to chauffeur him around. Pepper didn’t need to give up so much of her time and attention to him when she was overwhelmed with work. The researchers and employees at SI didn’t need some kid constantly exploding things. Dr. Cho could use the time that she would have spent patching up his one hundredth stab wound on more pressing issues. The Avengers didn’t need to have to look out for his bright blue and red spandex ass because he was the youngest and most inexperienced.

Peter had convinced himself that everyone was better off without him so he wouldn’t break down. He wanted all those things he couldn’t have anymore. He missed his friends and family like crazy. Although he knew that re-entering their lives would only expose them to danger and harm, he missed them like crazy.

The year after Strange erased everyone’s memory was the hardest.

He would often sit by May’s grave with fresh flowers and cry. Sometimes Peter had nightmares about her death. He would wake up screaming or drenched in cold sweat, or both. He wished for May to come back and hold him in her arms like he was a kid again, for her to kiss his brow and sing soft lullabies to him. When she had passed, Tony Stark had stepped in as an acting father of some sort to help sooth Peter’s grief and guilt.

How both of them were gone. The only people who gave a shit about his nightmares now were his neighbors, who would bang at this door and tell him to shut up at 2am.

Peter was overcome with crippling loneliness.

At least he had Karen.

He had taken a break from being Spiderman after Strange erased everyone’s memory. He no longer had educational records or a place to live.

That was what he told himself. The truth was that he was scared how much regret and guilt would hit him when he put on the suit that he had made so many memories in.

Crime had skyrocketed in the week he took off. Victims of mugging weren’t going to stop the mugging on their own anytime soon.

So Spiderman once again swung through the city, catching bike thieves and robbers alike.

When he had put his suit that Mr. Stark had made for him back on, he was surprised by a casual female voice in his ear.

“Welcome back, Peter” came the familiar robotic voice of Karen.

“Woah Karen? You still remember me?” Peter was astonished. Did the spell not work? What was going on?

A moment of silence.

“Why wouldn’t I remember you Peter?”

Relief flooded his veins. At least he still got Karen.

Karen remembered him because she wasn’t a sentient being. She was an AI. The data records of Peter had been removed but Karen remembered him. She might not remember who gave her to him or any connection back to Mr. Stark but she remembered him, Peter Parker. And that was good enough for the boy who had lost his world.

Chapter Text

Just like that, 4 years had gone by in the blink of an eye. Peter Parker was still a random loner at a community college and Spiderman was still an anonymous crime-fighting neighborhood vigilante. Separate lives, just like it was supposed to be.

Ironman and the Avengers were still going around fighting evildoers, Tony Stark was still tinkering in his lab, probably. Peter didn’t know anyways.

Ned and MJ were wrapping up their bachelors at MIT. Peter was so proud of them, he knew that his former best friends were overjoyed. He watched each and every one of their videos from his couch in his small dark apartment, a sad nostalgic smile on his face.

Peter was also almost graduating. The most challenging thing about college wasn’t the advanced science but rather finding a partner or a group for partner and group assignments. He was smart and respectful to the people in his group but he still had walls up around him. Jokes did float around with his project group occasionally about nerdy science pun jokes, but after the project, they all went back to their own devices. Them with their friends, and Peter by himself.

 

﹌﹌﹌﹌﹌﹌﹌﹌

When he walked into class on a regular morning, he was ready to take a nap. Crime fighting and loneliness had left Peter in a state of bone deep exhaustion, not to mention the new injuries that he had sustained. As long as he got enough nutrients and a fair amount of sleep, his body could heal those knife nicks or bullet grazes with ease.

As he trudged to his seat in the second-to-last row, he began to overhear conversations bubbling from his peers as normal. Usually, these conversations ranged from plans to hangout on the weekend to desperate attempts at persuading their friends to let them copy off their homework last minute. His enhanced hearing was quite annoying in that sense. He really didn’t care about whatever girls tend to gossip or ditch excuses why someone didn’t do their homework.

Today however, the conversations strayed off of their normal topics.

“Yo, did you hear about the news?”

Peter looked to the right and saw the kid who sat 2 seats away staring intently at him.

Quite confused, Peter asked what the news was.

“Apparently, our class got picked to go on a field trip!” The guy two seats away whisper-shouted, almost bursting with excitement. He delved into a semi-rant about how he heard rumors that they were going on a trip to Japan, or the Grand Canyon, or Stark Industries…

Wait what? Stark Industries? The Stark Industries with the Avengers and all that?

Peter didn’t like that. He had chosen Queens community college to keep a low profile. And, on top of that, every time he went on a field trip something bad happened. He got bit by that radioactive spider at Oscorp, the elevator at the Washington Monument almost killed everyone, the whole fiasco with Mysterio on the Italy trip, the list went on.

It probably wasn’t going to be some fancy trip, it was all rumors anyways. Probably like a walk down to the petting zoo because everyone was stressed over finals or something.

Peter really hoped it was a walk down to the petting zoo.

A small field trip like that couldn’t hurt right?

So when the professor announced that their class at Queen’s community college was chosen to take a tour of Stark Industries, Peter almost fell out of his chair.

The draw to go to Stark Industries was quite literally, one in a hundred thousand. All universities and colleges across the country had heard of the news and decided to enter the draw, but it was only Peter’s stupid luck that got his school picked.

“I told you!” The guy two seats to the right (Jeremy, was it..?) gloated.

If only it was a trip to the petting zoo. Peter scrubbed his hand over his face, it was way too early in the morning to be announcing that they were going to SI. He desperately hoped that it was still early in the morning so he misheard.

He did not mishear.

The TA began coming around to hand out a thick stack of NDA forms. Peter debated ripping the paper into pieces.

His mood was unmatched throughout the small class of 18 students. Everyone was pumped for this trip, afterall, it was basically a huge gamble. The dopamine rush from gambling must have hit them.

The trip was announced to be in two weeks, and they would be going with a couple other classes. They only had 18 students in their block of chemical engineering so they were going to be going with the mechanical engineering and the computer science classes too to make a combined giant class of 80 or so.

As expected, there was going to be a partnered assignment for the trip, because some people somehow were able to watch their phone and not pay attention for a whole trip to SI. Peter rolled his eyes.

Dr. Yarros, his professor, paired them off into twos. They had 18 people, an even number, so Peter was paired with Jeremy. Next to him, Jeremy was practically buzzing out of his seat with glee.

Peter felt bad. Jeremy didn’t have any friends in this class so if Peter bailed he would have to do the whole assignment and tour by himself. Jeremy had no friends within the classes going on the trip, well not anybody that Peter knew of.

Making Jeremy do the assignment by himself was better than potentially running into the Avengers and bringing back waves of painful nostalgic memories though, right?

He thought of what excuse would be good to tell his professor Mr. Yarros. Dentist appointment? Too common for an excuse. Family emergency? Why would he know he had a family emergency weeks before the field trip? Un-cancelable appointment to the therapist because of horrible anxiety? Yeah why not.

When he came up to Yarros at the end of class, he chickened out and forgot what he was going to say. Internal facepalm.

“Dr. Yarros, about the uh field trip to Stark Industries in two weeks, I, uhm” Peter stammered. He felt a flush creep up his neck.

He gulped.

“I… I’m not feeling well enough for it.” He ended up saying. It wasn’t a complete lie, Peter did truly not feel well. Although he hasn’t felt well in years since everyone forgot him.

Dr. Yarros fixed him with an incredulous stare before letting out a chuckle. “You’re funny! Parker was it?”

“I’m sure you’ll feel well once we tour SI, kid. Besides, it’s really a once in a lifetime opportunity.” He chuckled, patting Peter on the back. “I’m certain that you wouldn't want to miss it for anything in the world.”

If it was such a “one in a lifetime opportunity” as Yarros had said, Peter would have lived through 200 or more lifetimes. And Peter really did want to miss that damn field trip for anything in the world. Staying at home and working on his new web grenades were better than going on that trip by a thousandfold!

Peter had attempted to stammer something else, another excuse but Yarros wasn’t having it.

“Parker,” Yarros said firmly yet gently, “this field trip has a partner assignment worth 30% of your grade. If you skip both you and your partner gets a 0.” Yarros told him.

30%?! Which fool puts 30% of a kid and their partner’s grade on a stupid field trip? He thought back to how excited his classmate Jeremy had looked when he learned that it was a field trip to SI. And, it was basically free marks on the assignment.

Peter let out a groan internally.

The field trip really gnawed at Peter. On the way home, he worried about it. When showering, he worried about it some more. On his daily patrol around the city, his brain was going to burst from worrying about it.

Although it was a warm spring this year, Peter could feel a cool breeze against the ventilation holes in his suit. He tended to get sweaty when he fought evildoers around the city and Mr. Stark had added more efficient ventilation spots that could filter air when he saw Peter coming back with his hair almost drenched in sweat one day.

A notification from his phone interrupted his thoughts about Mr. Stark.

“Karen, show me the notification.”

Projected in front of him by his eye lenses, Peter saw that it was a notification from his Instagram.

Ned had posted another video. It was him and MJ, as always, talking about what they wanted to do after graduation. Ned wanted to become a content creator or a software developer and MJ wanted to do a degree in visual arts or pursue a possible Masters degree.

Peter let out a chuckle, maybe MJ was going to learn how to sculpt or oil paint people in crisis now.

He didn’t know what he wanted to do himself. His whole gimmick in fighting crime as Spiderman didn’t exactly pay him (he believed in non-profit saving the day work). After May’s death and his 18th birthday, he had inherited both her money and the money his parents had left him.

He looked back down to the video. The video was ending and Ned was thanking his followers for watching.

During the time when everyone knew Spiderman’s identity, Peter’s followers had skyrocketed. People flooded his DMs with gratitude for a while before all the hate and death threats had crashed in. Peter hadn’t been able to stomach all the messages from people asking him why he couldn’t save their grandmother, their parents, their child so he deleted the app.

Peter hated seeing those messages of blaming him for children orphaned because he couldn’t save their family in time. The public knew it was a sore spot for him as he had lost his own parents at an extremely young age; so they excessively attacked him there. It honestly almost hurt more than a stab wound. For years he had nightmares about the plane crash that took their lives. Luckily, his aunt May had been there to take care of him. Now, she was gone too.

After everyone’s memories were wiped. Peter was not surprised to find that he lost basically all his followers on social media. He added a couple classmates for group project discussions but that was really it.

Peter had Karen close his social media app — there was no use in dwelling in a past he couldn’t change.

The sun had already set, and lights from tall apartment buildings and streetlights now illuminated the dark city. The most prominent light however to nobody’s surprise was the SI tower, the bright A for Avengers a bright blue.

Peter jumped off the building and began to swing home.

It was going to be a long week before that damn trip.

“Uhm.. I forgot..?” He offered, shrugging his shoulders in what he hoped was a nonchalant way.

Tony Stark stared at him, an unreadable expression on his face.

“What flavor of pizza do you like?”

There was nothing wrong with Star Wars, was there? He suggested it and FRIDAY quickly pulled it up onto the large projector screen.

The sun began to reach the final stage of its setting in the west. Soft orange glows were cast into the room, spilling over Peter.

It felt nice to be at home again, surrounded by the people he loved.

Perhaps it was because he felt at home again, or because he had watched A New Hope several million times before, but he quickly dozed off.

When he woke again, the credits were rolling across the screen. Peter looked around – similarly, some Avengers had also dozed off. Loki was sniping the dirtiest pictures of Thor's gaping mouth caught in a snore.

He looked at Tony, who was also watching him.

“How was the nap, sleeping beauty?” Tony asked.

Peter blushed. It was so embarrassing to have been caught falling asleep in front of all of the Avengers.

He looked at his wrist for the time. It was close to 10 pm.

Peter looked back at Tony. He still hasn't told him his identity yet, and it was really now or never.

“Mr. Stark? Can I talk to you somewhere more private?” Peter asked quietly.

Tony nodded and Peter followed him as he led them to his lab.

“Shoot whatever you got to say, kiddo.”

It was now or never. Peter looked into the patient eyes of Tony Stark and in one fluid motion, took off his mask.

Peter had expected him to look shocked, angry, or even horrified but Tony Stark showed none of those emotions. Instead, his face lit up with the warmth of a smile. No anger, no shock, nothing.

Tony Stark grinned at him. “I began to add it all up a while ago. Your stab wound, your agility, your nervousness to accept my job position, it was quite obvious to me.”

“And then your voice was the last thing that tied it all together. I knew your voice sounded familiar, and I really did think about it before landing on you.”

“If you knew, why didn’t you confront me about it? Or told anyone else?”

“Peter, kid, I know how tough it is for you to wear the mask and always have to make the right choices when there aren’t obvious right ones. I wasn’t going to force you or anything. In every aspect, that is your choice to make, not mine.”

Peter felt tears swelling up.

“Kid, are you crying?” Tony asked. His face was drawn with worry. “Did I say something wrong?”

“It’s okay, underoos, it’s okay.” He rubbed circles on Peter’s back.

That made him cry even harder, how Tony called him underoos again.

“You’re getting snot and tears on my favorite shirt, you know that right?” He said jokingly.

Peter flushed crimson and tried to pull out of the embrace but Tony held him tightly.

“I’m just kidding roos, you can cry your snot and tears onto this old shirt anytime you want.”

Chapter Text

Tony held onto Peter as he sobbed for a long time, his hand rubbing gentle circles on Peter’s back to soothe him.

After he pulled away, he felt quite mortified. Mr. Stark barely knew him, and he had just sobbed his heart out in his arms. But Tony didn’t seem to care.

After a while, Tony asked about his friends that he had mentioned. About MJ and Ned. Peter felt a new wave of sadness at the mention of former friends.

Peter hadn’t thought of it like that. Maybe the excitement on Ned’s face whenever Peter talked about saving people as Spiderman or the underlying joy in MJ’s eyes behind the fear when Peter swung her around was all genuine.

“Peter, I think you should talk to them.” Tony said firmly. “It’ll only hurt you more the longer you have to wallow in regret and fear.”

Peter looked up at Mr. Stark, whose firm eyes stared back at him.

Eventually, Tony cajoled him into moving his stuff back into the tower. ‘Would make lab time more easier without having to commute’ he had explained.

Peter thought about it for a while before he agreed. Tony had a couple guys from the tower help Peter move his stuff into his room at the end of the hall as well as into a section of Tony’s lab.

Eventually, Peter even racked up the courage to tell the other Avengers that he was Spiderman. He missed going on patrols with them, the feeling of having each other’s backs in case there was a serious threat.

Most of them were absolutely incredulous. Peter trusted them enough to know that they would never reveal his secret. And they trusted Peter to know that he wasn’t of any harm to them.

 

﹌﹌﹌﹌﹌﹌﹌﹌

Tony dropped Peter off at MIT the next week. He had been building his confidence all week, but he was still super shaky. Mr. Stark had told him that it was okay to be nervous, and for him to give it his best shot.

Peter really did want to give it his best shot.

Come on, you’re Spiderman. You have faced way worse things than walking up to your former friends and telling them that you were their friend.

It was now or never.

“Hey, can I talk to you for a second?” He asked her. She looked up at him with guarded eyes.

“If you’re one of the tennis club members who is still trying to set your buddy up with me,” She began.

Peter quickly interrupted. “No, no! I’m not one of them.”

“Oh-kay? What do you need.”

When she finished, she gave the paper back to him.

“How do I know this isn’t a joke? I-I don’t even know who you are.” MJ slowly asked.

“I-I can explain in more detail if we go somewhere private?” Peter responded. He immediately realized how perverted that sounded. God, he just sounded like a creepy old man trying to lure her to a dark alleyway.

“I mean, like not in a weird way!” He stammered.

“Well, I’m heading home and I guess we could talk there, Peter” she offered.

They walked into MJ’s apartment to find Ned staring at a bulletin board. Specifically, a bulletin board with pictures of Spiderman connected to pictures of different locations and different people, just like how it looks in detective movies.

“MJ, I think I found a lead to who Spiderman is.” Ned said without glancing up.

“Ned, we have a…guest.” MJ announced.

“Yeah, he’s saved me and MJ countless times. Tell me that isn’t fate or something.” Ned added. “Spiderman is a friendly neighborhood hero, why was he at Washington and Vienna conveniently when we also were?”

Ned noticed him examining the board so carefully and came over to explain his thought processes.

“We think that he might be British, and so we’ve narrowed it down to these people.” He pointed to 3 different pictures. Peter really didn’t know whether to laugh or cry when he saw the picture of their high school science teacher Roger Harrington.

Turns out, there were a lot of people that wanted to meet Spiderman to thank him for his work over the years. Unlike how the people of New York had absolutely hated him enough to post vandalized pictures of his face onto NY Square billboards, they now were grateful and appreciative of the work he did around the city.

“The most recent sighting was here, by New Hyde Park, where Spiderman was seen stopping a mugging..” Ned went on.

Peter hadn’t expected Ned to look for him too, in his own way. He might have forgotten Peter, but he still searched for Spiderman.

“Hey Ned, Michelle,” Peter started.

They both turned to look at him.

Peter could hear his heartbeat in his ears. Nervous energy ran through him.

“What if I told you guys that I… was Spiderman.” He said anxiously.

“No way..”

“Hey Peter, was I anything more than a friend to you?” MJ asked cautiously as Peter was about to push open the door.

“You gave this to me in Italy, didn’t you?”

“Yeah, I did.” He admitted. “We used to be…lovers.”

MJ stared at the broken necklace in her hand. “I can tell that you really cared for her. The old MJ.”

“You know we probably won’t go back to what we were before, don't you? She asked softly.

Peter knew that, yet he had always held a small fragment of hope in his heart that somehow, miraculously, they would go back to how it was before everyone had forgotten him.

Now, hearing her words, he felt his heart shatter into pieces.

“Yeah, I know that.” He whispered.

“Hey roos, uh” He started, “you know how great it is to have you be part of the family here right?”

“Mr. Stark? What’s this about?”

“I was talking with Pepper, and we kind of need an heir for SI. And we don’t really want to have children anytime soon.”

“Oh, cool! Are you going to adopt a kid?” Peter asked curiously.

Tony rubbed his arc reactor self-consciously.

“Well, me and Pepper were hoping that you would be the heir. You’re basically already our kid, but we could legally adopt you? I mean, you already have an all-clearance pass.” He asked nervously. “Of course, if you don’t want that, that’s totally okay.” Tony had given Peter back his all-clearance pass and quietly apologized for the anxiety that he had caused Peter on the day of his field trip.

Peter was stunned. Tony and Pepper were asking to adopt him and make him the heir of SI? Was he dreaming?

Tony’s face melted into an expression of relief. “I was concerned that you were going to say no earlier, kid. You scared me.”

Tony held him back. “When will you ever stop calling me Mr. Stark and rather by my name, which is Tony in case you didn't remember?”

“Sorry old man.”Peter grinned fake bashfully.

"You little twerp." He said affectionately. Tony began to ruffle Peter’s hair despite his protests of how he had just washed it.

“Hey bambino, by the way, how did you have access to vibranium for your suit?”

“So my suit was actually gifted to me from a genius billionaire playboy philanthropist if you could believe it…”
